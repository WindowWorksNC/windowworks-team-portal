What is and is not covered

Submit within 30 days. Turn in each expense within 30 days of the date on the receipt. Later submissions are flagged and may not be reimbursed.

Covered when pre-approved and documented with a receipt: travel meals up to the federal rate for that location, lodging, parking and tolls, ride share for work travel, airfare and baggage, and approved supplies or materials for the job.

Not covered: alcohol, personal entertainment, room upgrades or extras beyond policy, traffic or parking fines, expenses for anyone who is not an employee, anything without a receipt, and any self-created marketing or branded material that was not approved in advance. Marketing over $100 needs Rose's approval before you spend.

Reimbursements are paid on the regular pay cycle after approval.
